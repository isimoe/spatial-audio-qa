Approach

Method Overview

A three-stage pipeline converts raw multi-channel microphone array recordings into spatially grounded representations for multimodal reasoning.

1

Array Recording

Multi-channel microphone array captures real-room acoustic scenes, preserving inter-channel time and level differences across overlapping speakers.

2

Localization & Separation

Spatial front-end estimates each source's azimuth trajectory and separates concurrent speech into clean, direction-tagged audio streams.

3

Spatial Info + Audio to Model

Explicit spatial coordinates and acoustic embeddings are jointly fed into the language model to answer direction-conditioned queries accurately.

Real-World Benchmark

Evaluation Suite Across Three Difficulty Tiers

Recorded in real acoustic environments with multi-channel arrays. Tasks progress from basic scene perception to temporal-spatial binding and target-speaker transcription under overlap.

Easy

Perception

“How many distinct speakers are talking, and where is Speaker 1 located?”

Scope: Source counting and coarse direction-of-arrival (DoA) localization in single- and multi-speaker scenes.

Medium

Spatio-Temporal

“Which direction is active between 2.0 s and 4.0 s, and who started speaking first?”

Scope: Active-time source selection, speaker turn ordering, and cross-speaker temporal comparison conditioned on azimuth.

Hard

Selective ASR & QA

“Transcribe only the speaker at −45° while ignoring the overlapping speaker on the right.”

Scope: Target-direction transcription and spatial counterfactual QA under concurrent speech overlap.
