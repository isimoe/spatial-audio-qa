Approach

Method Overview

A three-stage embodied pipeline turns raw multi-channel audio from a quadruped-mounted microphone array into ego-centric spatial representations for grounded reasoning.

1

Onboard Array Recording

A circular microphone array mounted on the quadruped's back captures ego-centric multi-channel room audio relative to the robot's heading (0°).

2

Localization & Separation

Spatial front-end estimates each speaker's relative azimuth around the quadruped and separates overlapping speech from room reverberation and ego-noise.

3

Spatial Info + Audio to Model

Ego-centric azimuth tokens and separated acoustic embeddings enter the language model to produce spatially grounded answers and robot turn/target cues.

Real-World Benchmark

Evaluation Suite Across Three Difficulty Tiers

Recorded in real indoor rooms using a quadruped-mounted multi-channel microphone array. Tasks progress from basic ego-centric perception to temporal-spatial binding and target-speaker instruction transcription under overlap.

Easy

Perception

“How many distinct speakers are talking, and where is Speaker 1 located?”

Scope: Source counting and coarse direction-of-arrival (DoA) localization in single- and multi-speaker scenes.

Medium

Spatio-Temporal

“Which direction is active between 2.0 s and 4.0 s, and who started speaking first?”

Scope: Active-time source selection, speaker turn ordering, and cross-speaker temporal comparison conditioned on azimuth.

Hard

Selective ASR & QA

“Transcribe only the speaker at −45° while ignoring the overlapping speaker on the right.”

Scope: Target-direction transcription and spatial counterfactual QA under concurrent speech overlap.
